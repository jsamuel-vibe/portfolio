I designed and built Cretile’s internal tool for safer campaigns and timely follow-ups.

How I found the problem

Every campaign meant piecing the workflow together again.

I ran campaigns and mapped replies with sales, so the friction was visible: changing Excel exports, repeated audience setup and follow-ups that waited for someone to be online.

How might we bring this work into one system without building the entire sales platform?
Product strategy

Replacing a campaign tool was not enough reason to build.

Losing our Meta account forced a new number—and a choice.

Option A

Reconnect the tool

Restore the same workflow.

Chosen

Build the first module

Own the workflow and future automation.

Use case 01 · Campaign operator

Launch the right campaign with confidence.

Job to be done

Before launching, I need to verify the audience and message—and keep a way back.

Built. Audience review, preview, opt-outs and undo.

Principle

Error prevention

Review the audience and message before sending.

Principle

User control and freedom

Undo makes the final action reversible.

What changedThe team is more relaxed at launch.

Use case 02 · Campaign operator

Keep follow-ups moving while I’m away.

Job to be done

When replies arrive, the right follow-up should not depend on me being online.

Built. Claude matches choices to follow-ups.

Principle

Cognitive offloading

The system remembers which follow-up each choice needs.

Use case 03 · Campaign operator and sales

Act without memorizing WhatsApp’s rules.

Job to be done

Before acting, we need to know what WhatsApp allows.

Built. Warm-up, quality and reply status in context.

Principle

Visibility of system status

Show eligibility and limits before action.

Principle

Recognition rather than recall

Keep WhatsApp rules inside the workflow.

The product today

One place to launch campaigns and continue conversations.

The live release joins contacts, campaigns, analytics and inbox safeguards for me and one salesperson.

Next direction · not yet built

Carry lead context forward from the first enquiry.

Next, the website, WhatsApp and sales can share lead context.
