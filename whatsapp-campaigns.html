Cretile: one sales workflow, starting with WhatsApp

I designed and built the first module of Cretile’s internal sales platform: one place for contacts, campaigns, replies and AI-assisted follow-ups.

Type
Internal product
Timeline
Two weeks
Role
Product design and build

The problem

When Cretile moved to a new WhatsApp number, the campaign setup had to be rebuilt. Lead lists lived in changing Excel exports, while conversations and follow-ups lived elsewhere.

Sending a message was not the real problem. Carrying context from one step of the sales process to the next was.

Product strategy

I started with the workflow the team needed immediately, but designed it as the first module of a broader sales platform—not a standalone WhatsApp tool.

Design decisions

01 Show the final message

The WhatsApp preview stays beside the form, so the operator can check the real message while building the campaign.

02 Keep opt-outs out

Opted-out contacts remain visible for context but cannot be selected again.

03 Give mistakes an exit

A short delay gives the operator one last chance to stop the wrong send.

04 Make WhatsApp’s limits visible

The product shows the reply window and warms the number gradually, pausing when Meta reports a warning.

A workflow we could shape

One campaign asked leads to choose from four services. Claude watched the replies and sent the matching follow-up for each selection, even while I was away.
