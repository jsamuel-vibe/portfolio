Design decisions

Six decisions made campaigns safer to run.

01

Tag at the point of entry

A required tag gives every imported audience usable structure.

02

See the message before sending

The final WhatsApp message stays visible while the campaign is assembled.

03

Opt-outs stay locked

Opted-out contacts stay visible but cannot be selected again.

04

A few seconds to undo

A short delay gives the operator one last chance to stop a send.

05

Make the reply window visible

The inbox shows whether WhatsApp’s 24-hour reply window is still open.

06

Warm the number without babysitting it

Daily limits rise gradually and pause when Meta reports a warning.

What I learned

Start narrow, but design for the system around it.

The first release did not need to solve the entire sales process. It needed to work today without closing the door on the product Cretile actually wanted.

That is the 0→1 work I enjoy: finding the useful first step, shipping it and using it to make the larger direction concrete.
